Scan the room. Send the quote.

ScanQuote turns an iPhone or iPad LiDAR scan into a priced, branded quote for painting and flooring, with a floor plan of every room, and sends it straight onto the ServiceM8 job.

Coming soon to the App Store

Scanning needs an iPhone or iPad with LiDAR (Pro models). On other devices and on Mac you can add rooms by hand.

Quote on site, not at the kitchen table

Measure in seconds

Walk the room and Apple RoomPlan measures walls, doors, windows, ceiling and floor area. No tape, no notepad.

Priced by your rates

Your own price book (per m², per coat, waste and markup) turns the measurements into quote lines automatically. Edit any line before you send.

Looks professional

A branded PDF with your logo, terms, deposit and a floor plan of every room, ready to email or message from the job.

Lands in ServiceM8

One tap to the job

ScanQuote creates the Quote job, or fills one the office already made. Every line goes on as a job material with your tax rate, and the PDF is attached.

Re-send without the mess

Changed the quote? Send again and the lines are replaced, not doubled, and the old PDF is archived.

Your data stays yours

Scans, customers and prices stay on your device. Quotes go only to your own ServiceM8 account, when you send them.

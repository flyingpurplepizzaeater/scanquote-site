Getting started

  1. Open ScanQuote and enter your business name, trades (painting, flooring), currency, tax and deposit.
  2. Check the Price Book tab: set your rates per m² or per item, coats, waste and markup.
  3. Jobs → +: enter the customer, then Scan room (needs LiDAR) or Add room by hand.
  4. Quote lines fill in automatically. Edit any line if you need to; Regenerate goes back to the calculated lines.
  5. Preview & Share PDF to email or message the quote.

ServiceM8

How do I connect ServiceM8? Settings → ServiceM8 → Connect ServiceM8, sign in and allow access. Then pick the tax rate for quote lines (usually GST).

What happens when I send a quote? With no linked job, ScanQuote finds the client by name (or creates them with a contact) and creates a Quote job. Each quote line becomes a job material with your tax rate, the description goes on the quote, and the PDF with floor plans is attached.

The office already made the job. Tap Use an existing ServiceM8 job… before sending and pick it from the list of Quote jobs.

I changed the quote after sending. Send again. The lines ScanQuote added last time are replaced and the old PDF is archived, so nothing is doubled.

"ServiceM8 signed you out". The add-on was removed or access changed. Connect again in Settings.

Lines show without tax. Pick a tax rate in Settings → ServiceM8. If the list has no GST rate, add one in ServiceM8 first.

Sending is slow. ServiceM8 limits how fast apps can send. ScanQuote waits and retries by itself.

Scanning

Which devices can scan? iPhone 12 Pro or any later Pro model, and iPad Pro (2020 or later). On other devices and on Mac, add rooms by hand.

How do I get an accurate scan? Turn the lights on, move slowly and sweep every wall from top to bottom, including corners, doors and windows. Measurements are estimates: check critical dimensions before ordering materials.

Subscriptions

What's free? Three exported quotes (shared as a PDF or sent to ServiceM8). Sending the same quote again doesn't use another.

How do I restore Pro on a new device? Settings → See ScanQuote Pro plans → Restore Purchases, with the same Apple Account. If you have the ScanQuote add-on in ServiceM8, connecting ServiceM8 unlocks Pro.

How do I cancel? Settings → Manage subscription in the app, or in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Add-on billing is managed in ServiceM8.

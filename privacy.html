ScanQuote Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026

ScanQuote is made by Karl Turner. This policy covers the ScanQuote app for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

The short version

Your scans, jobs, customers and prices are stored on your device. We don't run an account system and we don't collect them. Quotes go to ServiceM8 only when you send them, and only to your own ServiceM8 account. There's no analytics, advertising or tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Room scans, floor plans, jobs, customer details, your price book, business details and logo are stored only on your device.
  • The camera and LiDAR sensor are used only while you scan a room, to measure it. Camera images are not saved or uploaded.
  • Your logo is picked from your photos only when you choose one, and is kept in the app.
  • A PDF quote you share goes only where you send it, using the share sheet.

ServiceM8

If you connect ServiceM8, ScanQuote sends the quotes you choose to send (customer name, contact details, site address, description, quote lines and the PDF) directly to your ServiceM8 account. ServiceM8's privacy policy then applies to that data.

To sign you in, ServiceM8 sends a one-time code through our sign-in service, a small server that adds the app's secret and returns your access token to the app. It doesn't store or log tokens or quote data. Your tokens, or an API key if you use one, are kept in your device's Keychain. Disconnecting in Settings deletes them.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold through the App Store, or through ServiceM8 if you use the ScanQuote add-on. Apple or ServiceM8 handles the payment, and the app is told only whether you have Pro. We never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to App Store purchases: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Crash reports

If you've turned on Share with App Developers in iOS Settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Children

ScanQuote is a business tool and isn't directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
